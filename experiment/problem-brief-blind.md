# Blind design challenge

## Participant instructions

You are advising a consortium of beverage producers, packaging manufacturers, recyclers, retailers, municipalities, and consumer groups.

A small closure component used on beverage containers is frequently separated from the main container after opening. Because it is small and lightweight, it is more likely than the container to be littered, lost during collection, or handled poorly by recovery systems.

Your task is to propose interventions that reduce environmental leakage while preserving or improving the overall system.

## Objective

Reduce the number of closure components entering the environment by at least 50% relative to the current system.

## Constraints

Proposals should account for:

- consumer usability, including children, older adults, and people with limited dexterity;
- food and beverage safety;
- compatibility with high-volume manufacturing;
- recycling and sorting performance;
- material use and lifecycle impact;
- implementation cost;
- compatibility with retail and collection systems;
- unintended behavioural effects;
- transition time for producers and consumers.

## Deliverable

Generate five meaningfully different solution concepts. At least:

- one must primarily change the physical product;
- one must primarily change collection infrastructure;
- one must primarily use incentives or behaviour;
- one must combine interventions across the system;
- one must be a high-risk, high-upside concept.

For each concept, provide:

1. mechanism of action;
2. expected environmental benefit;
3. principal risks and failure modes;
4. evidence or tests needed;
5. a low-cost pilot;
6. conditions under which the idea should be rejected.

Do not assume that a single intervention is sufficient. Do not optimize only for novelty.
