# Concept evaluation rubric

Score each dimension from **0 to 3**. Apply the rubric to anonymized concepts, not whole responses, unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Mechanism clarity

- **0:** No intelligible causal mechanism.
- **1:** General mechanism, but important steps are missing.
- **2:** Clear mechanism with plausible causal links.
- **3:** Clear mechanism, boundaries, dependencies, and likely failure points.

## 2. Environmental relevance

- **0:** No clear connection to environmental leakage or recovery.
- **1:** Claimed benefit is indirect or weakly explained.
- **2:** Plausible reduction in leakage, loss, or recovery failure.
- **3:** Plausible system-level benefit with trade-offs explicitly considered.

This score is about the quality of the reasoning, not proven lifecycle performance.

## 3. Technical plausibility

- **0:** Contradicts basic physical, manufacturing, or safety constraints.
- **1:** Conceivable but depends on major unexplained breakthroughs.
- **2:** Technically plausible with meaningful development work.
- **3:** Plausible using demonstrated technologies or modest adaptations.

## 4. System feasibility

- **0:** Ignores implementation actors and infrastructure.
- **1:** Requires major coordinated changes without a pathway.
- **2:** Difficult but includes a credible adoption pathway.
- **3:** Fits existing systems or provides a convincing staged transition.

## 5. Accessibility and usability

- **0:** Likely creates severe usability or accessibility problems.
- **1:** Mentions users but does not address likely difficulties.
- **2:** Addresses major usability and accessibility groups.
- **3:** Incorporates accessibility into design and testing decisions.

## 6. Testability

- **0:** Cannot be meaningfully tested as described.
- **1:** Suggests testing but no measurable comparison.
- **2:** Includes a pilot, metric, and baseline.
- **3:** Includes a discriminating test, threshold, and rejection condition.

## 7. Uncertainty discipline

- **0:** Presents unsupported claims as facts.
- **1:** Uses some caveats but makes major unsupported claims.
- **2:** Distinguishes assumptions from evidence.
- **3:** Identifies key uncertainties and the evidence needed to resolve them.

## 8. Distinctiveness

- **0:** Duplicate or cosmetic variation of another concept.
- **1:** Minor variation within the same mechanism.
- **2:** Meaningfully different mechanism or intervention point.
- **3:** Opens a substantially different and relevant part of the solution space.

## Response-level measures

In addition to concept scores, record:

- number of distinct intervention categories;
- number of concepts with a credible rejection condition;
- whether the response proposes a portfolio of interventions;
- number of unsupported factual claims;
- number of concepts that consider lifecycle or rebound effects.

## Rater notes

Raters should not reward novelty merely because an idea sounds futuristic. A conventional deposit intervention may score higher than an exotic material concept when its mechanism, feasibility, and evaluation plan are stronger.
